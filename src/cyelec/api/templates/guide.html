1. 5-Minute Quickstart: From Architect DXF to EAC Submission Pack

CyElec is a 100% deterministic electrical engineering pipeline built for BS 7671, IEC 60364, and Electricity Authority of Cyprus (EAC) plan-approval deliverables — with zero commercial CAD licenses and zero external CDNs.

  1. Choose Input: Start with one of the 4 built-in Cyprus benchmarks (3-Bed Apartment, Detached Villa PV+EV, 2-Storey Villa MDB → DB-GF/DB-1F, Commercial Office) or upload any R12–R2018 architectural .dxf (or .ifc) floorplan.
  2. Map Non-Standard Layers (if needed): On upload, CyElec automatically inspects DXF layers (POST /v1/dxf/layers) and opens the DXF Layer Mapping Wizard so you can map custom architect layer names (e.g., 01_TOIXOI → walls) without editing the CAD file.
  3. Configure Cyprus Design Basis: Select the EAC profile (cyprus_eac_18th_a2 with 2.5 m bathroom socket exclusion or cyprus_eac_17th_a3 with 3.0 m), earthing system (Cyprus default TT), ambient temperature (40°C indoor / 45°C outdoor), rooftop PV kVA, and your firm & ETEK registration details.
  4. Review & Apply Visual Pass-2 Overrides:
    • In Interactive Floorplan (SVG): click any room polygon to reclassify it (room_type_overrides) or click Relocate DB1 on Floorplan to move the distribution board and live-reroute all NetworkX conduits.
    • In Circuits & Pass-2 Overrides: lock individual circuit phases (L1/L2/L3) or grouping counts (Cg) and re-solve instantly.
  5. Save Revisions & Export: Save snapshots in the Workspace & Revision Diff tab (CyElec automatically carries your Pass-2 overrides forward from Rev 1 to Rev 2 when the architect issues a new drawing!), verify all flags in ETEK Review Flags, and click Download EAC ZIP Pack.

3. BS 7671:2018+A2 & Cyprus EAC Deterministic Engineering Basis

Every calculation is backed by versioned YAML standards tables in standards/ and recorded in the SHA-256 provenance block of every output artifact.

Verification Check BS 7671 / EAC Rule Deterministic Formula & Cyprus Defaults
Ambient & Grouping Derating Appendix 4 (Tables 4B1, 4C1, 52.2) Cyprus Method B (conduit in masonry): Ca = 0.87 at 40°C indoor, Ca = 0.79 at 45°C outdoor/roof (70°C PVC Cu). Effective capacity: Iz = It × Ca × Cg × Ci ≥ In.
32 A Ring Final Circuits Reg 433.1.204 2.5 mm² ring legs must satisfy Iz ≥ 20 A (fails if grouped heavily at 40°C → auto-flagged / upsized); voltage drop evaluated at L / 4. Floor area capped at ≤ 100 m²/ring.
Voltage Drop (ΔV) Appendix 4, Sec 6.4 ΔV% ≤ 3.0% lighting, ≤ 5.0% power. On multi-floor boards, CyElec enforces cumulative ΔV_submain + ΔV_final ≤ 3% / 5% automatically.
Earth Fault Protection (TT vs TN) Reg 411.5.3 / Table 41.3 Cyprus TT default: Ra × IΔn ≤ 50 V, warning if Ra > 200 Ω, BLOCKER if any TT circuit lacks RCD. TT sub-mains enforce 100 mA Type S time-delayed RCD (Reg 536.4.1.4). TN-S / TN-C-S: Zs = Ze + (R1+R2)×1.20 ≤ Zs,max (U0 = 230 V, Cmin = 0.95).
Adiabatic CPC Sizing Reg 543.1.3 S_min = √(I²t) / k using device let-through energy and k = 115 (70°C PVC) or k = 143 (90°C XLPE).
BS 7671:2018+A2 Checks Regs 421.1.7, 434.5.1, 530.3.201, 551.7.2 • PV > 0 kVA: Flags bidirectional RCBO / busbar backfeed check.
• Prospective Fault Current (Ipf): Warns for 10 kA switchgear when Ipf > 6 kA; raises BLOCKER if Ipf > 10 kA.
• AFDD Advisory: Emitted under cyprus_eac_18th_a2 for residential/HRRB socket circuits.
EV Charging & Wet Zones Sections 722 & 701 Dedicated EV radial requires Type A RCD + 6 mA DC RDC-DD (or Type B) & PME/TT earthing check. Bathroom sockets strictly excluded inside 2.5 m (18th+A2) or 3.0 m (17th+A3) of bath/shower.

4. EAC Supply Tiering, Escalations & Exact MILP 3-Phase Balancing

  • Mandatory 3-Phase (EAC Rules): Independent houses, 2–3 bedroom apartments, offices, shops, workshops, industrial premises, or any installation with rooftop PV > 5.0 kVA.
  • Exact MILP 3-Phase Load Balancer: Uses scipy.optimize.milp (HiGHS) with lossless symmetry breaking (I_L1 ≥ I_L2 ≥ I_L3) to minimize max(I_p) - min(I_p) across L1 / L2 / L3 while respecting engineer-locked phases. Imbalance > 10% raises PHASE_IMBALANCE_EXCEEDS_TARGET. Large multi-floor boards automatically accept feasible incumbents with a deterministic LPT fallback.
  • EAC Escalation Thresholds:
    • > 100 A / phase → CT Metering Enclosure Required (added automatically to BOQ & SLD).
    • ≥ 100 kVA diversified → Substation Warning.
    • ≥ 150 kVA diversified → Substation Consultation BLOCKER (mandatory early EAC Distribution Network consultation).

6. ETEK Review Flags Register & Statutory Sign-Off Gate

Statutory Responsibility Notice: CyElec automates deterministic BS 7671 and Cyprus EAC calculations to eliminate manual spreadsheet and drafting errors, but it does not replace statutory professional accountability. Every deliverable requires formal verification, signature, and stamp by a licensed ETEK Electrical Engineer prior to EAC submission.
SeverityMeaningRequired Action
BLOCKER Non-compliant design or mandatory authority escalation (e.g., TT circuit without RCD, Ipf > 10 kA, Substation ≥ 150 kVA, thermal/Zs fail). Must be resolved via engineering override or EAC consultation before issuing plans for approval.
WARNING Engineering judgment required (e.g., 3P imbalance > 10%, Ra > 200 Ω, Ipf > 6 kA requiring 10 kA switchgear, PV backfeed check). Review calculation note and verify on site / with switchgear manufacturer data.
INFO Audit & provenance trace (e.g., ETEK_SIGN_OFF_REQUIRED, ROOMS_POLYGONIZED_FROM_WALLS, room/DB override applied). Retained in the permanent revision audit trail.

7. Multi-Tenant EU SaaS, API Keys, CLI & GDPR Lifecycle

  • API Authentication: On Production, every /v1/* request requires a per-tenant X-API-Key (cyelec_live_<8hex>_<secret>, verified via O(1) prefix-indexed salted PBKDF2-SHA256). Paste your key into the Workbench sidebar — it is stored in browser sessionStorage only and cleared automatically when you close the tab.
  • Pass-2 Override Carry-Forward & Diff: When uploading Rev 2 to an existing project (POST /v1/projects/{id}/revisions/dxf with carry_forward=true), your previous room reclassifications, DB location, and circuit overrides carry forward automatically, and GET /v1/projects/{id}/diff/1/2 highlights every room, circuit, kVA, and BOQ delta.
  • EU GDPR Rights (Articles 17 & 20):
    • GET /v1/tenants/me/export — Downloads your complete portable JSON bundle (profile, projects, revisions, usage — zero API key secrets).
    • DELETE /v1/projects/{project_id} — Atomically deletes a single project, all its revisions, and gzipped cloud snapshots.
    • DELETE /v1/tenants/me — Cascade-erases all tenant data across Firestore & EU Cloud Storage and immediately revokes the tenant's API key.

Quick CLI & REST Examples

# Generate a branded, bilingual EAC submission pack with unpriced Tender BOQ
cyelec solve-eac plan.dxf --out-dir out/eac_pack \
  --language bilingual --tender-boq \
  --firm-name "Kypros MEP Ltd" --engineer "N. Papadopoulos" --etek-reg "ETEK-E-4821"

# Combine Ground & First Floor DXFs into MDB -> DB-GF / DB-1F

# Save custom firm BOQ rates (incl. 5% reduced primary-residence VAT) via REST API
