1. 5-Step ETEK Engineer Workflow

  1. Select a Cyprus benchmark or upload architectural .dxf/.ifc (select 2+ .dxf for Multi-Floor).
  2. If layers are non-standard, map them in the DXF Layer Mapping Wizard.
  3. Set EAC Profile (18th+A2 2.5 m or 17th+A3 3.0 m), TT earthing, PV kVA, 19%/5% VAT, Tender BOQ, and firm/ETEK branding.
  4. Click Run Deterministic Solve, then refine visually on the Interactive Floorplan (click rooms to reclassify across 20 residential & commercial types, or click Relocate DB1 to re-route conduits).
  5. Resolve any BLOCKER flags, save revisions in Workspace, and click Download EAC ZIP Pack (with original DXF underlay & native PDF).

2. Cyprus EAC & BS 7671 Key Limits

  • Ambient & Cables: 40°C indoor / 45°C roof; 1.5–35 mm² 70°C PVC & 50–240 mm² 90°C XLPE/SWA + 80–400 A MCCBs & Type D motors.
  • Voltage Drop (cumulative): ≤ 3.0% lighting, ≤ 5.0% power (sub-main + final combined) + 45% conduit/tray fill.
  • TT Earthing (Cyprus default): Ra × IΔn ≤ 50 V, 30 mA RCD on finals, 100 mA Type S selective RCD on TT sub-mains, plus live-conductor adiabatic check (Reg 434.5.2).
  • Commercial & Wet Zones: BS 5266 Emergency Lighting, BS 5839 Fire Alarm spur, Server UPS, 3P VRF/Lift feeders, and Sec 701 2.5 m / 3.0 m exclusion.
  • EAC Escalations: 3P mandatory for houses/2-3 bed flats/PV > 5 kVA; CT metering > 100 A/ph; Substation warn ≥ 100 kVA, blocker ≥ 150 kVA.

3. Messy DXF & Bilingual Know-How

  • AutoCAD Blocks: Nested INSERT blocks are exploded recursively with Layer "0" inheritance & fixture hints.
  • Missing A-ROOM? Closed rooms are automatically derived via wall-to-room polygonization.
  • EN / EL / Greeklish labels: Recognizes ΣΑΛΟΝΙ, SALONI, ΚΟΥΖΙΝΑ, KOUZ., ΥΠΝΟΔΩΜΑΤΙΟ, YPN. 1, ΜΠΑΝΙΟ, MPANIO, ΒΕΡΑΝΤΑ, ΠΛΥΣΤΑΡΙΟ.
  • Multi-Floor DXFs: Combine GF.dxf + 1F.dxf via POST /v1/solve/dxf/multifloor or cyelec solve-multifloor.

4. Custom BOQ, Tender Mode & Governance

  • Custom Firm Rate Card: Save your firm's EUR unit rates & 5% reduced VAT once via PUT /v1/tenants/me (boq_rate_overrides).
  • Unpriced Tender BOQ: Set tender_boq_blank_rates=true (or --tender-boq) to leave Excel Column G blank while preserving live contractor bid auto-calc formulas.
  • Statutory Gate: All outputs carry SHA-256 provenance and require formal review & stamp by a licensed ETEK Electrical Engineer.
  • EU GDPR: Export all tenant data via GET /v1/tenants/me/export or erase completely via DELETE /v1/tenants/me.
